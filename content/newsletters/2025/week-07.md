## 163.2 and Nobody Was Close

On Like McConkey Kong put up 163.2 on Team Kirkland, which is not just the best score of week 7 but the best score anyone has posted all season. Bo Nix went for 40.0 against a 16.7 projection. Ja'Marr Chase added 38.1. When your quarterback and your top receiver both clear anything the rest of the league managed at their position, the other 40-odd points are just decoration.

Kirkland's 121.9 would have beaten four teams this week. It got buried by 41.2 instead, the largest margin of the slate. That's the difference between a bad week and a bad draw, and Kirkland got the second one on top of the first.

The win moves McConkey Kong to 4-3 with a three-game streak and fourth in true strength. Fourth is the last playoff spot. They're playing like they intend to keep it.

## Puka-chu Left 49.7 on the Bench

Christian McCaffrey scored 39.1 for Puka-chu, the best running back performance in the league this week. Puka-chu lost by 14.7.

Here's how. Mike Evans started at the flex and scored 0.0 against a 14.0 projection. Travis Hunter sat on the bench and scored 24.1. Zach Charbonnet also sat, for 19.5. Total damage: 49.7 points left behind, a 68.2% lineup efficiency, the worst in the league this week. The optimal lineup was 156.4. Finding Deebo scored 121.5.

So it wasn't close, it was a choice. Deebo, for their part, earned it honestly with Trey McBride at 29.4 and the Patriots defense at 20.0, and they're 4-3 now with Puka-chu right behind at the same record but ranked seventh in all-play against Deebo's fifth. Puka-chu has already been running 0.91 wins ahead of what their scoring deserves. Weeks like this are how the ledger gets settled.

## Kirkland's Tight End Slot Deserves Its Own Investigation

Darren Waller started at tight end for Team Kirkland and scored 0.0, the worst mark at the position in the league. Theo Johnson scored 15.6 from the bench. That's 15.6 gone in one roster line.

Sam Darnold added 8.6 at quarterback, the lowest total any started QB managed anywhere. Kirkland finished with 32.1 on the bench and a 154.0 optimal. Even that wouldn't have beaten 163.2, which is the only consolation available and it isn't much of one.

Meanwhile Dillon Panthers benched Justin Herbert and his 27.9, the best quarterback left unused all week, and lost by 33.6 to Never Gonna Gibbs You Up. Third straight loss.

## The Unluckiest Team in the League Is Still Outside the Cut

Never Gonna Gibbs You Up scored 143.4 and put out a perfect lineup — 100.0% efficiency, nothing wasted, the only manager in the league who got it exactly right. They have scored more points than any team this season. Their all-play record is 56-21, first in true strength. Their actual record is 3-4.

That gap is 2.09 wins worse than their scoring earned, and it's the single largest injustice in the standings. Six weeks left. If the schedule ever stops targeting them they'll climb fast.

At the other end, Vegas Vikings sit first at 5-2 on a five-game winning streak, and they got there this week despite starting the Steelers defense for negative four points. Beating hit it fergie 🕺 by 19.5 while one of your roster spots actively subtracts from the total is a kind of flex. Gang of Platipy is right behind at 5-2 with the second-best all-play mark, having dispatched Not so Goated, who scored 97.1, the lowest of the week, and are now 1-6 on a six-game skid with the fewest points in the league.
